# Brief

Kiln Hollow is a potters' hamlet in a narrow valley of red clay. The ground is packed clay worn into terraces, with sage scrub on the banks and a slow, silty stream along the valley floor. Bottle kilns of fired brick stand against the hillside with their fire-mouths glowing; drying racks, stacked urns and a glaze-seller's stall line the lanes between them, and a potter and her goat work the yard. The morning sun comes over the eastern rim from the top left, warm and dusty; smoke drifts from the kilns. Colours are earthen (clay, sage, silt-green water, smoke-grey stone) with one bright accent: the blue glaze, used sparingly.

## The pieces

- **clay** (tile, 24x24): packed red clay with fine cracks and a few pale pebbles
- **urn** (sprite, 24x24, about 14 px tall): a squat terracotta urn with a dark rim
- **kiln** (object, 24x48, about 44 px tall): a bottle kiln of fired brick, its fire-mouth glowing at the foot
